Prices since 1913: official inflation vs. metals

Each line shows how many times its 1913 price something cost in a given year (1913 = 1×). The scale is logarithmic, so the same rise anywhere on the chart means the same percentage change.

Price multiplier since 1913

Combined is a basket that held equal dollar amounts of gold, silver and copper in 1913 (the average of their three multipliers). Gold has no 1960 value in the source data, so the gold and combined lines have a one-year gap there.
